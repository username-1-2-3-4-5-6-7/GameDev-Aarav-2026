PROJECT BRIEF

What the explorer does.

  1. Uses a full-window HTML5 canvas with a black background.
  2. Renders vibrant, smoothly cycling colors that remain vivid as you zoom.
  3. Shows a dashed preview of the exact region that will fill the screen.
  4. Uses the mouse wheel to resize the preview and change the zoom scale.
  5. Zooms into the preview region when you click the canvas.
  6. Includes a clickable hint box with an explanation of the formula.
  7. Uses descriptive camelCase JavaScript names beginning with my, async/await, and direct event property assignments where possible.
